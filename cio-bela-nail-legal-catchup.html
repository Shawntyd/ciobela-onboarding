Document 01

Shared Station Agreement

The Collaborative Vision

We are building the nail department of Cio Bela together. This agreement ensures our shared station remains professional, hygienic, and inspiring for every artist.

1. Professionalism & Aesthetic

  • Station Presentation: Each nail tech is responsible for keeping their designated area organized, clean, and visually aligned with the salon's brand.
  • Atmosphere: We agree to maintain a professional tone and a welcoming, private environment for all clients.
  • Client Privacy: The nail station is an intimate service environment. We commit to ensuring client privacy and dignity at all times.

2. Shared Station Stewardship

  • Common Areas: The restroom and waiting area are shared. We agree to "leave it better than we found it."
  • Cleanliness: Adhesive residue and used supplies must be addressed immediately after each client. Never leave residue for the next artist.
  • Turnover: Every nail tech is responsible for fully sanitizing the nail station, tray, and surrounding surfaces between every appointment.

3. Logistics & Access

  • Entry: Each artist will have keyed access. Ensure the building is fully secured when the last person leaves.
  • Client Flow: If a client arrives early, guide them to the designated waiting area — not the nail station.
  • Friends & Family: Friends and family are not permitted to occupy the salon unless they are present to receive a service.

4. Cancellations & Calling Out

As an independent business owner, you are fully responsible for managing your own schedule and client communications. The salon reserves the right to terminate your rental agreement if cancellation standards are not upheld consistently.

5. Texas Compliance (TDLR)

We collectively agree to keep the station "Inspection Ready" at all times. Your current license and government-issued photo ID must be available for inspection at all times.

Document 02

Daily Stewardship Checklist

Opening Procedures

  • Lights & Atmosphere: Turn on station lighting. Set music at appropriate volume.
  • Station Prep: Verify the nail station is fully sanitized, tools are disinfected, and supplies are stocked.
  • Restroom Check: Verify it is clean, stocked, and trash is empty.

Between Every Client

  • Station Reset: Remove and dispose of all used single-use items immediately. Replace before the next client.
  • Surface Sanitation: Wipe down the nail station, trolley, and shared surfaces with EPA-registered disinfectant after every appointment.
  • Product Care: Cap all polishes and products tightly between clients.
  • Hand Hygiene: Wash hands or use 70% alcohol sanitizer before the next client.

Closing Procedures

  • Full Surface Wipe-Down: Final sanitation of all surfaces and tools.
  • Adhesive Storage: Store all products in a cool, dry container per manufacturer instructions.
  • Waste Disposal: Empty station waste bin into main dumpster.
  • Final Walkthrough: Lights off, station is clean, door is locked.
Document 03

Texas Safety & Compliance Protocols

1. Licensing & ID (Texas Law)

  • Visibility: Your current Texas Cosmetology or Nail Technician license must be displayed at your workstation in public view at all times.
  • Identification: You must have a government-issued photo ID available for TDLR inspection at any time.

2. Client Intake & Contraindications

  • Pre-Service Consultation: Every new client must complete a client intake form documenting known allergies, medications, and skin conditions.
  • Patch Tests: Strongly recommended for all new clients.

3. Infection Control & Sanitation

  • Single-Use Strict Policy: Nail files, buffers, and other single-use items are strictly single-use.
  • Reusable Tools: Metal implements must be fully sanitized/autoclaved with an EPA-registered disinfectant between every client.

4. Chemical Safety

  • Ventilation: Adequate ventilation must be maintained at your station at all times when using acrylics, gels, or other chemical products.
  • MMA Ban: Use of MMA (methyl methacrylate) monomer is strictly prohibited per TDLR regulations.

5. Blood Exposure Procedure

  1. Stop the service immediately.
  2. Flush the affected area with clean water for 15 minutes.
  3. Apply antiseptic and cover if needed.
  4. Document the incident and notify Cio Bela management immediately.
  5. Clean and disinfect all tools before resuming.
